QR Code Attendance Management System

Technology plays an important role in improving efficiency in education. Traditionally, school records the students attendance manually by calling out the names of students or written sheets, which can consume time and may result in recording errors. One possible alternative is the use of QR codes, which allows attendance to be recorded digitally by scanning using phones or compatible devices. With the use of this technology, teachers may be able to manage attendance records more easily and reduce the time spent on checking students presence. It can also help organize attendance information digitally, making records easier to access and monitor

This study aims to determine the effectiveness of QR code scanning on attendance recording efficiency at St. Paul Christian School. Specifically, it will examine whether QR code scanning can be efficient at recording attendance, improve accuracy, and provide a faster way of monitoring the students attendance.

Step by Step Procedure:

Step 1: Prepare Your QR Code

Prepare your assigned unique QR Code for identification

Step 2: Scan Your QR Code

Position your unique QR Code in front of the camera until it is successfully detected

Step 3: Verify Your Information

Wait for the system to recognize and display your student information

Step 4: Confirm Your Attendance

Once your information is verified, the system will record your attendance along with the corresponding date and time
